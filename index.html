Who We Are

Rooted in Faith.
Built for Excellence.

Saint Anthony of Padua's Catholic Comprehensive College (SAPCCOL) is a co-educational boarding and day school in the Diocese of Kumbo , North West Region, Cameroon. Founded under the patronage of the Capuchin Friars, SAPCCOL blends rigorous academics with a deep Catholic tradition of character formation.

Situated on a serene highland plateau in Mbohtong-Shisong, the campus offers a distraction-free environment for serious academic and spiritual growth. Students from across Cameroon come knowing that SAPCCOL means discipline, excellence, and faith.

Capuchin Franciscan Heritage

Co-Educational Boarding

Both day and boarding options available. Boarding students benefit from structured dormitory life, pastoral care, supervised study periods, and a strong peer community.

Serene Highland Location

Set 3 km from Shisong Catholic Hospital on the plateau above Kumbo Council forest — peaceful, safe, and purposefully removed from urban distractions.

Technical Facilities

Dedicated labs for Electrical Power Systems and Civil Engineering, plus computer labs for ICT students in Forms 4, 5, and High School.

College Refectory

A communal dining hall where all students eat together. Each student receives a plate, cup, and spoon upon arrival. Standard institutional meals are served.

Labour & Campus Care

Students participate in campus upkeep as part of formation — carrying a rag, broom, and rubber boots for scheduled labour periods that build discipline.

What to Bring

Student Requirements

Every student — day or boarding — is expected to arrive fully prepared. Below are the complete requirements for the school year.

🦮

Personal Hygiene Items

  • A dozen of toilet tissue / handkerchiefs
  • One or two plastic bucket(s)
  • A personal container to preserve drinking water
  • Bathing slippers, toothbrush and toothpaste
  • A dozen of savon (very important)
  • A dozen of bathing soap / rubbing oil / comb / foot brush
  • A floor rag & a towel
  • A durable bathing bag (not a soap dish)
👓

Dresses & Clothing

  • 2 thick night dresses — preferably thick tracksuit & long thick black socks (NB: Nights are very cold)
  • At least 2 compound wears (sample in school)
  • Inner wears / underwears (at least a dozen — very important for hygiene)
  • White dressing slippers (crocs) for girls, black for boys
  • NB: No unwanted dresses or gadgets shall be allowed in school
👔

School Uniforms

  • School T-Shirt — obtained in school at 2,500 frs
  • Two Coffee Brown 4-pleat skirts with band (girls) / Two Coffee Brown trousers (boys)
  • Sport shoes (preferably white)
  • Sports wear: Yellow/Red (1st Cycle), White (2nd Cycle) — available at 3,500 frs
  • Thick hooded compound wear Pullover — sold only in school at 7,500 frs
  • Two+ pairs of school sandals (sobo) & black low-heel shoes only
  • School daily-wear Pullover — 5,000 frs (in school)
  • White Kinclerks
  • School Identity Card — 1,000 frs (pay Day 1)
🙏

Devotional Requirements

  • A Cameroon Hymnal or School Hymnal
  • A Good News Bible, Rosary, or Simple Prayer Book
  • Praying the Psalms Daily — sold in school at 1,500 frs
🔧

Labour Items

  • A rag & a bundle of broom
  • A pair of rubber shoes or rain boots for labour
  • All personal property should carry the student's name
🛌

Boarding — Beddings

  • An 8-spring bed mattress
  • 4 bed sheets: 2 sky blue & 2 of any colour of your choice
  • 2 pillow cases & a pillow
  • A thick blanket (NB: very cold here)
  • A trunk with good locks
  • Charcoal iron, an umbrella and/or a rain coat
  • A solar torch or battery torch with spare batteries
🍽

Refectory

  • A plate, cup and spoon to be given to the school once (if misplaced/destroyed, student replaces)
  • All students are encouraged to eat what is provided at table
  • The college kitchen has no provision for special meals or supplements

Admissions Process

We welcome students of faith, discipline, and academic ambition. Here is everything you need to apply for the 2026/2027 school year.

📋 How to Apply

  • 1

    Gather Your Documents

    Collect all required documents — birth certificate, baptismal card (for Catholics), report cards, and result slips for Lower Sixth entry.

  • 2

    Pay the First Installment

    Make the first fee payment at AFG Bank or OPSECS Bank Cameroon. Boarding: 100,000 frs. Day students: 40,000 frs. Keep your receipt.

  • 3

    Submit at the School Office

    Bring all documents and your bank receipt to the SAPCCOL admissions office at Mbohtong-Shisong, P.O. Box 11, Kumbo.

  • 4

    Confirmation & Orientation

    Upon successful admission, you receive your school calendar, uniform details, and boarding or day student orientation information.

📁 Required Documents

  • ✓ Photocopy of Birth Certificate
  • ✓ Photocopy of Baptismal Card (for Catholic applicants)
  • ✓ Report Cards or Transfer Certificates and other relevant documents
  • ✓ Result Slip — required for admission into Lower Sixth
  • ✓ Payment receipt of the first installment of fees
